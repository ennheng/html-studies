PLATE 00 · THE EDGE OF THE KNOWN WORLD
City I · The city of thresholds

Ondine

where every door opens onto water

In Ondine, the streets are canals and the canals are mirrors. At dawn, residents lower their doors on brass chains and step directly into boats. No one says goodbye; they say, “May your reflection arrive before you.”

Coordinates: north of a half-remembered dream
Population: 8,412 and their reflections
The morning ferry, departing a bedroom window
City II · The city of measure

Cassira

where shadows are the only currency

At noon, when every citizen is briefly penniless, Cassira holds its great market. Towers are built tall enough to cast generous afternoons. A narrow house may make a family rich, provided it stands beside someone patient.

Coordinates: fifteen degrees east of yesterday
Chief export: borrowed time
The Exchange, five minutes before noon
City III · The city of return

Virelai

built in circles around a vanished bell

Virelai has no outskirts. Every road curves gently home, though no traveler agrees on where home is. Once each year the empty bell tower is filled with pigeons, and their wings make the sound the city has been missing.

Coordinates: exactly one turn from here
Local custom: arrive without announcing it
The silent tower, waiting for its annual voice
City IV · The last city

Novalume

which rebuilds itself from each arrival

You will not find Novalume as you left it. Every visitor brings one window, every departure takes a stair. Yet the bakery remains on the corner, and the baker remembers exactly how you take your morning bread.

Coordinates: at the edge of this sentence
Found whenever you are ready
A familiar window, in an unfamiliar tower
